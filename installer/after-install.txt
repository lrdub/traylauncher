TrayLauncher is installed.

To put it on your taskbar:
1. Open the Start menu and find TrayLauncher (type its name).
2. Drag it onto the taskbar.
   If dragging does not work, right-click it, choose Open file location,
   right-click TrayLauncher.exe, choose Show more options, then Pin to taskbar.

Click the pinned icon to open the menu. Choose "Edit apps list" in the menu
to change which apps appear.
